Bundled fonts retain their SIL Open Font License 1.1 notices. Font files and
licenses live in `public/assets/fonts/`. The editorial font manifest records
package versions, source URLs, weights, and files for Noto Sans SC, Noto Serif
SC, ZCOOL XiaoWei, Ma Shan Zheng, DM Sans, Cormorant Garamond, and JetBrains Mono.

retrieved by a site owner remains subject to the original rights holder's
terms and is stored in the owner's local, Git-ignored media library.
